# WEBSITE SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Between:**
Neo Steinhoff ("Service Provider")
and
Christina Steinhoff ("Client")

**Date:** 9 June 2026

---

## 1. SERVICES

The Service Provider agrees to design, develop, and maintain a professional website for the Client at christinasteinhoff.com, including:

- Full website design and development (custom Next.js build)
- Hosting setup and domain management
- Ongoing SEO optimisation (monthly)
- Content updates as requested (up to 2 per month)
- Performance monitoring and maintenance
- Technical support via WhatsApp

---

## 2. COMPENSATION

The Client agrees to compensate the Service Provider as follows:

### One-Time Payment (Due upon website launch):
- **1× Rolex Day-Date** (reference to be agreed between parties), OR equivalent agreed retail value in AED
- **1× Van Cleef & Arpels Bracelet** (style to be agreed between parties), OR equivalent agreed retail value in AED

*Both items to be delivered in original box with papers/certificate of authenticity.*

### Monthly Retainer (commencing month after launch):
- **AED 1,000 per month**
- Covers: SEO optimisation, Google Analytics reporting, content updates, technical maintenance, hosting oversight
- Payable by the 1st of each month via bank transfer

---

## 3. TIMELINE

| Milestone | Timeframe |
|---|---|
| Website design approval | Within 7 days of agreement signing |
| Development complete | Within 14 days of design approval |
| Website launch | Within 21 days of agreement signing |
| Monthly SEO retainer begins | Month following launch |

---

## 4. OWNERSHIP

- The Client retains full ownership of the website, domain name, and all content
- Upon termination, the Service Provider will transfer all assets and credentials to the Client within 7 days
- The Service Provider retains the right to reference this work in a portfolio (with Client approval)

---

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All custom code, design, and copy created for this project is owned by the Client upon full payment of the one-time compensation.

---

## 6. SEO SERVICES (Monthly)

The monthly retainer includes:
- Keyword research and on-page optimisation
- Meta titles and descriptions updates
- Google Business Profile management
- Monthly performance report (traffic, rankings, leads)
- Schema markup implementation
- Page speed optimisation

*The Service Provider cannot guarantee specific search rankings, as these are determined by Google's algorithms.*

---

## 7. HOSTING & DOMAIN

- The Client is responsible for hosting costs (estimated AED 0–150/month depending on plan chosen)
- The Client is responsible for domain renewal (approx. AED 50–80/year)
- The Service Provider will manage technical setup at no additional charge

---

## 8. TERMINATION

- Either party may terminate this agreement with **30 days written notice**
- The monthly retainer ceases upon notice period completion
- The one-time payment (items) is non-refundable once the website is launched
- If the Client terminates within 6 months of launch, a pro-rated buyout fee of AED 3,000 applies

---

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties agree to keep the terms of this agreement confidential. The Service Provider will not share Client data, business information, or personal details with any third party.

---

## 10. LIABILITY

The Service Provider's total liability is limited to the monthly retainer fees paid in the preceding 3 months. The Service Provider is not liable for lost revenue, downtime caused by third-party hosting, or Google algorithm changes.
